## Project note

Average Poem Machine examines AI poetry and Portuguese as a lived language rather than a neutral output format. Repeated words become motifs, line length affects duration, and different pitch and timing ranges make generic or culturally situated language perceptible through contrast.

The browser experience plays one poem at a time and provides a shared stop control. Its purpose is not to rank poems, but to make the assumptions inside the mapping discussable.
