## Project note

The listening lab turns reverb evaluation into a spatial, embodied comparison. Visitors can move through a compact 3D scene, switch audio routing modes, ping a source, and inspect RT60, wet/dry, and performance values through the HUD.

The current environment is intentionally labeled work in progress: Godot's built-in reverb remains a stand-in while native plug-in integration is developed.
